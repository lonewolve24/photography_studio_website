This Cookie Policy explains how Shotz uses cookies and similar technologies on our website.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site work properly and can remember preferences.

How we use cookies

We group cookies on this site as follows:

Essential cookies

These are required for the website to function. They include cookies used for security (such as CSRF protection) and basic session handling. These cannot be switched off through our site.

Preference cookies

We store your cookie consent choice locally in your browser so we do not show the consent banner on every visit.

Third-party content

Some pages may load embedded content from third parties, such as Google Maps on our contact page. Those providers may set their own cookies when their content is loaded. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies for more information.

Managing cookies

When you first visit our site, you can accept or decline non-essential cookies using the cookie banner. You can also clear or block cookies through your browser settings at any time.

Please note that blocking essential cookies may affect how parts of the website work.

Updates

We may update this Cookie Policy if our use of cookies changes. The latest version will always be available on this page.

More information

For details on how we handle personal data, see our Privacy Policy. Questions? Contact us.
